The formula

Four actives. Dosed to the research.

Four actives on a base of electrolytes, each at the amount studies actually use. No proprietary blends and nothing hidden behind "a blend of."

5g

Creatine monohydrate

Performance · Recovery
What it does

Your most explosive moves run on ATP, and your muscles burn through it in about ten seconds. Creatine keeps the backup tank full. More phosphocreatine on hand means ATP gets rebuilt faster, and your fifth sprint looks a lot more like your first.

Why this amount

5 g a day is the dose used across decades of research and backed by the International Society of Sports Nutrition.

2g

Beta-alanine

Performance
What it does

Know the burn in the last minute of a drill? That's acid piling up in your muscles. Beta-alanine gives them more carnosine to soak it up, so the burn hits later and you get more out of every hard set.

Why this amount

Studies use 3.2 to 6.4 g a day. Each QUBE has 2 g, so one or two a day fits the research and avoids the tingle a big single dose can cause.

1,000mg

Tart cherry extract

Recovery · Inflammation
What it does

Feeling sore and weak the day after a big lift is your muscles dealing with oxidative stress and tiny bits of damage. Anthocyanins, the pigments that turn tart cherries deep red, help clear that stress so your strength comes back sooner.

Why this amount

Standardized for anthocyanins at 1,000 mg, a well-studied dose in the middle of the range used in recovery research.

500mg

Curcumin, from turmeric

Inflammation · Recovery
What it does

Hard training flips on NF-κB, a switch inside your cells that releases inflammatory signals like IL-6 and TNF-α. Curcumin turns that switch down, supporting a healthy inflammatory response and less soreness after tough sessions.

Why this amount

500 mg sits in the range used in exercise recovery studies, where curcumin lowered inflammation markers and post-game soreness in athletes.

Na · K · Mg

Electrolyte blend

Hydration
